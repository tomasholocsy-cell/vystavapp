Vystavapp privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Vystavapp is an invoicing app for a few people who were given it personally. It has no accounts, and its maker runs no server.

Your data stays on your device

Your companies, clients, invoices and settings are stored only on the device you use. The maker of Vystavapp cannot see them. They are part of your own backup of the device (iCloud, Time Machine or a computer backup), if you have one switched on. Invoices, exports and backup files you save or send go only where you put them. Vystavapp never sells or shares your data. To delete everything, delete the app; on a Mac also delete the folder Library/Application Support/Vystavapp. Files you saved or sent stay where you put them.

No analytics

Vystavapp has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app itself sends no usage data to anyone.

Lookups

When you enter a company ID or a VAT ID, or ask for an exchange rate, Vystavapp asks a public register directly from your device: ARES (Czech company register), RPO (Slovak company register), VIES (EU VAT number check) and the Czech National Bank (exchange rates). It sends only what the lookup needs: the company ID or VAT ID being checked, or a date. A VAT check also sends your own VAT ID, so that the answer counts as your proof of the check. Each register sees your internet address.

Gmail, only if you connect it

If you connect a Gmail account, Vystavapp can send your invoices and reminders from it, and only when you press Send. It asks Google for two things only: permission to send email (gmail.send) and your email address (openid, email), which it shows so you know which account is connected. It cannot read, search or delete your mail. Each message, with its recipients and attachments, goes directly from your device to Google and through no one else. The sign-in is stored encrypted on your device and nowhere else; your address and Google's number for each sent message are kept with the invoice on your device. You can disconnect at any time in the app, which deletes the sign-in, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Vystavapp's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Updates and test builds

The Mac app checks GitHub for a newer version and downloads it from there; GitHub sees your internet address, as with any download. If you install the iPhone app through TestFlight, Apple gives the maker crash reports, your iPhone model and iOS version, how often the app was opened, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. That comes from TestFlight, not from Vystavapp, and it does not include your invoices unless a screenshot you send shows them.

Contact

Vystavapp is made by a private person in the Czech Republic. Questions: vystavapp@gmail.com
