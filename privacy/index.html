Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

The short version

Vystavapp keeps your data on your device. The developer runs no server, collects no analytics and receives none of your data.

What Vystavapp stores

Your invoices, clients, companies, saved items, logos and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's own folder. Backups you export are files you save wherever you choose.

Gmail

Connecting Gmail is optional. If you connect it, Vystavapp asks Google for three permissions only:

  • Send email on your behalf (gmail.send), used only when you send an invoice or a payment reminder;
  • your email address (openid, email), used only to show which account is connected.

Vystavapp cannot read, search or delete your email. The sign-in token is encrypted and stored only on your device. Messages go directly from your device to Google. They never pass through anyone else. You can disconnect in the app at any time, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Vystavapp's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Public lookups

When you use these features, your device asks public services directly. Only the value needed for the lookup is sent:

  • a company ID to the Czech business register (ARES) or the Slovak register (RPO);
  • a VAT ID to the EU VIES service;
  • a date to the Czech National Bank for an exchange rate.

Sharing

Vystavapp never sells or shares your data. It sends data only where you tell it to, for example an invoice to your client.

Contact

Questions: vystavapp@gmail.com

Česky ve zkratce

Vystavapp ukládá všechna data jen ve vašem zařízení. Vývojář neprovozuje žádný server, nesbírá analytiku a vaše data nedostává. Propojení s Gmailem je volitelné. Aplikace pak smí jen odesílat e-maily a zjistit vaši adresu, schránku nečte. Přihlašovací token je šifrovaný a uložený jen ve vašem zařízení. Přístup můžete kdykoli zrušit v aplikaci nebo na myaccount.google.com/permissions.
