Vystavapp

Invoicing for Czech and EU freelancers. Vystavapp is a Mac app that keeps all of your invoices, clients and companies on your own device. Nothing is stored on our servers, because there are none.

If you connect your Gmail account, Vystavapp can send invoices and payment reminders from your own address. It only asks Google for permission to send email. It never reads your mailbox.
